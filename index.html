About Me

Hi! I'm Claire, I'm an 8th grader, and I like performing and music. I also like mysteries, (Minecraft ARGs, murder mysteries, horror, etc) while liking pretty things at the same time, (building aesthetic kits like Rolife, sparkly things like rocks and jewels, and locking in). It's fun to share things I like and things I'm good at with others to and help them. My main focus right now is locking in and completing things I want to finish over the summer, and I'm constantly working on something I want to accomplish so my room won't be a mess forever😌

I also believe that music and performing can express emotions and connect people. Playing in orchestra, performing in theatre, and creating my own masterpieces can all do something like this, and I love doing it!
